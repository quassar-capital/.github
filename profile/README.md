# Quassar Capital

## About Quassar Capital

Quassar Capital is a New York–based hedge fund investing across global financial markets. Founded in 2012, the firm combines fundamental research, quantitative analysis, and investment judgment, with close attention to liquidity, market structure, and execution.

Our investment activities span distinct strategies, supported by specialized expertise and a shared commitment to disciplined decision-making.

## Technology at Quassar Capital

Financial markets generate vast amounts of information. Making sense of that information requires more than computational capacity: it demands sound research methods, an understanding of market behavior, and the judgment to distinguish meaningful signals from noise.

At Quassar Capital, technology supports the examination of financial data, the evaluation of portfolio exposures, and the study of how markets respond to changing conditions.

Our areas of technical interest include:

- **Quantitative Research:** Statistical analysis, factor relationships, and computational methods for examining financial markets.
- **Risk Analytics:** Portfolio sensitivities, exposure measurement, stress testing, and scenario analysis.
- **Market Microstructure:** Liquidity, price formation, trading conditions, and execution dynamics.
- **Data Engineering:** The organization, validation, and processing of financial information for analytical use.
- **Event Analysis:** The study of market responses to economic releases, corporate developments, and other consequential events.

These disciplines inform our research while remaining subject to the practical limitations of data, models, and market conditions.

## Investment Strategies

### Global Macro & Equities

Fundamental company analysis informed by macroeconomic developments, valuation, liquidity, and the broader forces shaping global public markets.

### MERIDIAN®

A multi-asset strategy employing a risk-parity framework to evaluate portfolio exposures, diversification, and changing relationships across market environments.

### Fill Alpha

An investment approach focused on identifying opportunities that may not be fully reflected in market prices.

### AXIS®

A systematic investment approach drawing on quantitative research, mathematical models, and the analysis of market behavior and execution dynamics.

### Venture Capital

Investments in emerging companies, financial technologies, and infrastructure opportunities, conducted through the firm's proprietary investment activities.

## Open Research & Engineering
